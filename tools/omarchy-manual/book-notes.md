# Omarchy Manual

<https://omarchy.org/manual/>

## Coming from Mac or windows

- Super is the center of everything. Super+K shows keybindings. Super+Space is
  "main" menu. Super+Alt+Space is Apps.
- Super+1/2/3/4 switch workspaces. Super+Shift+1/2/3/4 send windows to
  workspaces.
- Super+C/Super+V work everywhere. Super+Ctrl+V is your paste history.

## Navigation

- Super+L cycles through desktop mode; Super+Alt+L does in reverse.
- When you get to floating, Super+M is minimize nd Super+Alt+F is maximize.
- Super+F is fullscreen. Super+Alt+F is full-width. And Super+Ctrl+F is
  full-screen within a window (good for YouTube).
- Super+G starts a Group and every Window you open while that's active will
  belong to the group. Super+Ctrl+Arrow Left/Right will move between grouped
  windows, and Super+Alt+1/2/3/4 will go directly to grouped windows in order.
  Super+Alt+G will pop a window out of a group. And Super+G again will dissolve
  the group. You can also move windows from outside the group into it with
  Super+Alt+Arrows.
- Super+O will pop out a window to pin it as a floating window that follows you
  to other workspaces.
- Super+S is the scratchpad workspace, and Super+Alt+S moves stuff to the
  scratchpad workspace. It's good for controls or a terminal.
- Super+Grave is the quake terminal. And Super+Shift+Grave sends a window to
  join the quake terminal. Send it away with Super+Shift+Number.

## The Top Arrow

- You can right click audio to mute, or scroll on the workspaces to go to
  next/prev workspace. You can right click agents to launch an agent.
- There are some questionably useful shortcuts for the top bar panels.
  Super+Ctrl+1-9 toggles the nth panel in the right section. And
  Super+Ctrl+A/N/B/D/P toggle Audio, Network, Bluetooth, Display, and Power.
  Super+Ctrl+Alt+D/E toggles Calendar and World Clock. After you get up in the
  Panel, arrows move between elements in the panel, enter activates, tab steps
  to the neighboring panel, and esc closes.
